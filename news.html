January 6, 2026
Our warmest congratulations to Zhiguang to have his work on the inherent leakage of hyrophobic gating officially accepted for publication in PRX Life! This work provides novel insights into the likely physical basis of so-called intrinsic openning of BK channels, a mysterious property that has been known for decades but poorly understood. Zhiguang made the discovery several years back but the publication of this important work was unfortunately delayed due to several unforeseen factors. We are extremely pleased that we finally have closure on this project! (UMass News Release)

September 19, 2025
Congratulations to Shanlong for getting his paper on iConRNA accepted for publication in PNAS! This is such an unique model with great potentials in studies of RNA structure, dynamics and phase separation. It was a huge effort to design and optimize the model through many iterations over the course of ~two years. The model can be accessed directly from GitHub https://github.com/lslumass/iConRNA. This work is supported by NSF. You also read more about this work through UMass News Release.

July 9, 2025
Congratulations to Zhiguang for having his direct simulations of voltage activation of BK channels accepted in its final form in eLife. Ultra-long simulations performed using Anton 2 supercomputer were able to directly sample voltage activation of the Core-MT BK channel, and revealed important insights into noncanonical voltage sensor movements and coupling to the pore. This study was mostly completed back in 2022, but its publicaiton was delayed due to various reasons. We are so glad that we are finally able to share with the community our findings!
